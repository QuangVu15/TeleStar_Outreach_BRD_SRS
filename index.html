Featured project

Redesign of the “TeleStar Outreach” Pipeline

Redesigned a multi-client lead outreach management system on the Bigin CRM platform, moving from a split-by-team layout to a single pipeline split by client.

Role
Lead Gen / Sales Ops · hands-on system redesign
Platform
Bigin CRM
Scale
~17 clients across markets

Problem

01

The old system split the pipeline by sales team, causing duplicate leads on entry.

02

No traceable filtering history, so old leads could not be recycled after 6 months.

03

No way to assess inbound lead quality or attribute it to a source.

Solution design

  • Consolidated into one single pipeline split by client; reps distinguished by Owner instead of a separate branch, avoiding fragmentation.
  • Added a quality-scoring field set (Fit level T1/T2, email check, industry, size), separating quality from progress.
  • A multi-channel outreach funnel (LinkedIn, Email, Calls, Zalo/WhatsApp) converging on one metric: the qualified meeting.

My contribution

  • Entered and cleaned inbound leads together with the Operations team.
  • Owned the qualification check via each social channel per country market.
  • Analyzed the root cause, redesigned the pipeline, wrote the documentation and operating conventions.

Value delivered

  • A single source of truth per client, less duplication.
  • Lead quality scored against each market's own standard, consistent across countries.
  • Proposed a roadmap to clean up technical debt (label/value mismatch, duplicate stages).
BA deliverable: A full 12-page BRD/SRS specification covering As-is/To-be, coded functional requirements, business rules, use cases, data dictionary and a traceability matrix.
